About E-Library

E-Library is a free digital reading platform created by Padam Kishore to make useful books easier to discover, open, and read on phones and laptops. The library focuses on clean book pages, readable PDF and EPUB formats, and a smooth browsing experience for students, readers, and lifelong learners.

Every public book page is designed to be discoverable by search engines, while reader-only and admin pages stay out of search results. This helps people find available books directly from Google and then continue reading inside the E-Library experience.
